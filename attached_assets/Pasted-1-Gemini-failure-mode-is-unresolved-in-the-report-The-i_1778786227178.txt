2. The in-memory `JOBS` dict is a demo risk. Jobs are stored in memory. On Replit, the server restarts between sessions and the container can be recycled. If a judge tries to download a file 10 minutes after the upload, the job_id will 404. Either persist jobs to Replit's built-in DB (one line with `replit.db`) or show a note in the UI that the download link is session-scoped.

Two things to do: first, add a Walrus connectivity check to `/api/health` so you know before a demo whether it's up. Take a phone photo, upload it, copy the before/after EXIF output, and keep that screenshot.
